THE IDEA

What should I look for?+

Masking is usually strongest near the masker and falls as probe frequency moves away. The pattern may be asymmetric: for a tone masker, masking can spread farther toward higher frequencies. Compare curve widths at 500, 1,000, 2,000, and 4,000 Hz as a qualitative view of auditory filtering and critical-band behavior.

These are your detection thresholds, not calibrated sound-pressure levels. Room noise, headphones, fit, attention, and the 2AFC staircase all affect the result. Add a no-mask baseline at matching probe frequencies to estimate masking amount.
